A junior engineer's first-week ticket was a small, low-risk bug fix, the
kind of task that mostly exists to give someone a pull request to open.

I wanted that first ticket to actually teach ownership, instead of just
teaching how to open a pull request and wait for review.

So I laid out the symptom instead of the fix and asked the junior to find
the cause. Then I sat in on the debugging instead of handing over the
answer the moment they got stuck.

The bug took longer to fix than it would have taken me to fix directly,
but the engineer understood the system afterward in a way no review
comment would have taught them.
